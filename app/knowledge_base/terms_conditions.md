## 1. Definitions

For the purpose of these terms and conditions, the following definitions apply:

- **"Lender" / "Company" / "Tata Capital"**: Tata Capital Financial Services Limited, a Non-Banking Financial Company registered with the Reserve Bank of India (RBI Reg. No.: N-13.02306).

- **"Guarantor"**: A person who guarantees the repayment of the loan in the event of default by the borrower.
- **"Sanction Letter"**: The formal document issued by Tata Capital specifying the approved loan amount, interest rate, tenure, repayment schedule, fees, and conditions.

- **"EMI"**: Equated Monthly Instalment — the fixed monthly payment towards loan repayment comprising principal and interest components.
- **"NPA"**: Non-Performing Asset — a loan account where the borrower has failed to make payments for 90 days or more.
- **"TCRPLR"**: Tata Capital Reference Prime Lending Rate — the internal benchmark rate used for floating-rate loan products.
- **"SARFAESI Act"**: Securitisation and Reconstruction of Financial Assets and Enforcement of Security Interest Act, 2002.
- **"CIC" / "Credit Bureau"**: Credit Information Company — organisations like CIBIL (TransUnion), Experian, CRIF High Mark, Equifax that maintain credit records.

## 3. Interest Rate

### 3.1 Fixed Interest Rate

- The interest rate remains unchanged throughout the loan tenure.
- The EMI amount and total interest payable are fixed at the time of disbursement.
- Pre-payment and foreclosure charges may apply (refer to the Schedule of Charges).

### 3.2 Floating Interest Rate

- The interest rate is linked to Tata Capital's internal benchmark rate — TCRPLR.
- The applicable rate = TCRPLR + Spread (fixed for the loan tenure).
- When TCRPLR changes, the effective interest rate changes accordingly, resulting in a change in EMI amount or tenure.
- Tata Capital will communicate rate changes and revised EMI/tenure to the borrower via SMS, email, or letter.
- TCRPLR is reviewed and announced periodically (typically quarterly). Historical and current TCRPLR rates are published on the Tata Capital website.

### 3.3 Rate Reset Mechanism

For floating-rate loans:
- Rate resets occur on the date specified in the loan agreement or on a standard reset date (e.g., the first of each calendar quarter following a TCRPLR revision).
- The borrower will receive a revised repayment schedule reflecting the new rate, effective from the next EMI due date following the reset.

### 3.4 Interest Computation

- Interest is computed on a **daily reducing balance** basis (i.e., interest for each day is calculated on the outstanding principal at the beginning of that day).
- Monthly interest = Sum of daily interest amounts for the month.
- This method ensures that any part-payment or principal reduction is immediately reflected in the interest calculation.

## 4. Repayment

### 4.1 EMI Payment Mechanism

- EMIs are payable on the due date specified in the Repayment Schedule (typically a fixed date each month — e.g., the 5th, 10th, or 15th of each month, as agreed).
- The primary mode of EMI collection is through **NACH (National Automated Clearing House)** mandate registered on the borrower's designated bank account.
- Alternative modes: ECS (Electronic Clearing Service), Standing Instruction to the borrower's bank, or post-dated cheques (PDCs) — as agreed.
- The borrower must ensure sufficient cleared funds in the designated bank account on or before the EMI due date.

### 4.2 Advance EMI & Pre-EMI Interest

- **Advance EMI**: For certain products, 1–3 advance EMIs may be collected at the time of disbursement. These are adjusted against the last EMIs of the tenure.
- **Pre-EMI Interest (PEMI)**: For under-construction Home Loan disbursements (released in tranches), the borrower pays only the interest on the disbursed amount until the full loan is disbursed and full EMI commences. PEMI payments do not reduce the principal.

### 4.3 Payment Application Order

Each EMI payment is applied in the following order:
1. Overdue charges and penal interest (if any)
2. Accrued interest
3. Principal repayment
4. Prepaid insurance premium (if applicable)

### 4.4 Change of EMI Due Date

The borrower may request a change in the EMI due date by submitting a written request. Tata Capital may approve the change subject to operational feasibility, and a revised NACH mandate may need to be registered. Administrative charges may apply.

## 6. Default, Consequences & Recovery

### 6.1 Events of Default

An "Event of Default" includes, but is not limited to:
- Failure to pay any EMI on the due date.
- Failure to pay any other amount due under the loan agreement.
- Breach of any term, condition, representation, or warranty in the loan agreement.
- Providing false, inaccurate, or misleading information in the application or at any time during the loan tenure.
- Bankruptcy, insolvency, or winding-up proceedings initiated against the borrower.
- Deterioration in the borrower's financial condition that, in Tata Capital's reasonable assessment, impairs the ability to repay.
- For secured loans: Damage, destruction, depreciation, or unauthorised transfer of the secured property.
- Any other event or circumstance that, in Tata Capital's assessment, materially affects the borrower's creditworthiness or the security of the loan.

### 6.2 Consequences of Default

Upon an Event of Default, Tata Capital reserves the right to:
1. **Demand immediate repayment** of the entire outstanding amount (principal + interest + charges).
2. **Levy penal charges** as per the Schedule of Charges.
3. **Report the default** to all Credit Information Companies, adversely affecting the borrower's credit score.
4. **Classify the account as NPA** if EMIs remain unpaid for 90+ days, as per RBI norms.
5. **Initiate recovery proceedings**, which may include:
   a. Engagement of Tata Capital's empanelled recovery agents (who shall act in compliance with the RBI's Guidelines on Recovery Agents)
   b. Filing of civil suit for recovery of dues
   c. Filing of criminal complaint (in cases of wilful default, fraud, or cheque dishonour under Section 138 of the Negotiable Instruments Act)
   d. Initiation of proceedings under the **Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC), 2016** (for corporate borrowers)
6. **For secured loans**: Initiate action under the **SARFAESI Act, 2002**, including:
   a. Issuance of demand notice under Section 13(2)
   b. Taking possession of the secured asset under Section 13(4)
   c. Sale or auction of the secured asset to recover dues

### 6.3 RBI Guidelines on Recovery

Tata Capital strictly adheres to the RBI's Fair Practices Code and the Indian Banks' Association (IBA) guidelines on recovery agents:
- Recovery agents will carry proper identification and authorisation letters.
- Agents will not resort to intimidation, threats, or harassment.
- Contact with the borrower will only be during reasonable hours (7:00 AM – 7:00 PM).
- The borrower has the right to file a complaint against any recovery agent who violates these norms.

## 7. Security & Collateral (Secured Loans)

### 7.1 Creation of Security

For secured loans (Home Loan, Loan Against Property), the borrower is required to create a security interest (mortgage) in favour of Tata Capital over the property offered as collateral. This mortgage is typically created by:
- **Registered Mortgage (Equitable)**: Deposit of original title deeds with Tata Capital along with a memorandum of deposit.
- **Registered Mortgage (by Registration)**: Execution and registration of a mortgage deed at the Sub-Registrar's office.

The form of mortgage will be determined by Tata Capital based on the property type, location, and applicable state laws.

### 7.2 Obligations of the Borrower Regarding Security

The borrower must:
- Not sell, transfer, lease, rent out, gift, or create any third-party charge or lien on the secured property without Tata Capital's prior written consent.
- Maintain the property in good condition and bear all maintenance expenses.
- Keep the property adequately insured against fire, earthquake, flood, and other risks (where required by Tata Capital).
- Pay all property taxes, municipal levies, and statutory charges on time.
- Immediately inform Tata Capital of any damage, destruction, encroachment, or legal dispute affecting the property.
- Provide access to the property to Tata Capital or its authorised representatives for inspection or valuation at any time during the loan tenure.

### 7.3 Release of Security

Upon full repayment and closure of the loan:
- Tata Capital will release the mortgage / charge on the property.
- Original property documents will be returned to the borrower within 30 days of loan closure.
- A No Objection Certificate (NOC) and lien release letter will be issued.
- For registered mortgages, Tata Capital will provide the necessary documents for filing the satisfaction of charge with the Sub-Registrar / Registrar of Companies (as applicable).
