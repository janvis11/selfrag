# Tata Capital — Privacy Policy & Data Protection Disclosure

*Last updated: February 2026 | Version 3.4 | Classification: Customer-Facing*
*Regulatory Framework: Information Technology Act, 2000 | Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA), 2023 | RBI KYC/AML Directions*

---

## 1. Introduction & Scope

Tata Capital Financial Services Limited ("Tata Capital", "we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting the privacy and personal data of its customers, applicants, and website/app visitors. This Privacy & Data Protection Disclosure outlines our practices regarding the collection, use, storage, sharing, and protection of personal and financial information.

This policy applies to:
- All individuals who apply for, hold, or have held any loan, credit facility, or financial product from Tata Capital.
- All visitors and users of Tata Capital's website (www.tatacapital.com), mobile applications, and digital platforms.
- Any person whose personal data is collected, received, or processed by Tata Capital in the course of its business operations.

## 2. Types of Data Collected

### 2.1 Personal Identification Data
- Full name, date of birth, gender, nationality
- PAN (Permanent Account Number)
- Aadhaar number (collected only for e-KYC with explicit consent, in accordance with the Supreme Court of India's Aadhaar judgement)
- Passport number, Voter ID, Driving Licence number
- Passport-size photographs
- Signature (physical and digital)

### 2.2 Contact Information
- Residential address (permanent and current)
- Office / business address
- Mobile phone number(s)
- Landline number(s)
- Email address(es)

### 2.3 Financial Data
- Bank account details (account number, IFSC code, bank name)
- Bank statements (transaction history)
- Income details (salary slips, ITR, audited financials)
- Existing loan and credit card details (including outstanding amounts and repayment track record)
- Credit bureau reports (CIBIL score, Experian report, etc.)
- Investment and asset details (provided by the applicant)

### 2.4 Employment & Business Data
- Employer name, designation, department
- Employment vintage and nature (permanent, contract, self-employed)
- Business name, registration details, turnover, vintage
- Professional qualifications and certifications (for professional loans)

### 2.5 Property Data (for Secured Loans)
- Property address, type, age, measurement, market value
- Title documents, encumbrance records, legal opinions
- Builder/developer details, project RERA registration
- Property tax and municipal charge details

### 2.6 Digital & Technical Data
- IP address, browser type, operating system, device information
- Cookies and tracking data from website and app usage
- App usage analytics (screen views, feature interactions)
- Location data (only when explicitly permitted by the user in the mobile app)

### 2.7 Communication Records
- Records of phone calls with customer care (calls may be recorded for quality, training, and dispute resolution purposes)
- Email correspondence
- Chat transcripts (from chatbot and live chat interactions)
- Written complaints, requests, and grievances

## 3. Purpose of Data Collection

Tata Capital collects and processes personal data for the following purposes:

### 3.1 Loan Processing & Underwriting
- Evaluating loan applications and creditworthiness
- Performing KYC (Know Your Customer) verification as mandated by RBI
- Conducting credit bureau checks (CIBIL, Experian, CRIF High Mark, Equifax)
- Verifying identity, address, income, employment, and business details through internal and external agencies
- Performing field investigation, property valuation, and legal title verification (for secured loans)
- Assessing risk and determining loan terms (amount, rate, tenure)

### 3.2 Account Servicing & Operations
- Disbursing loan amounts to designated bank accounts
- Collecting EMIs through NACH/ECS
- Sending account statements, interest certificates, and repayment schedules
- Processing prepayment, part-payment, and foreclosure requests
- Issuing NOCs and closing accounts
- Managing NACH mandates and cheque/payment instrument handling

### 3.3 Regulatory & Legal Compliance
- Fulfilling RBI KYC/AML (Anti-Money Laundering) and CFT (Combating the Financing of Terrorism) obligations
- Reporting to Credit Information Companies (CICs) as mandated by the Credit Information Companies (Regulation) Act, 2005
- Filing statutory returns with RBI, tax authorities, and other regulators
- Complying with court orders, legal notices, and law enforcement requests
- Maintaining records as required by the Prevention of Money Laundering Act (PMLA), 2002
- Meeting audit and compliance requirements

### 3.4 Customer Communication
- Sending transaction alerts, reminders, and notifications related to the loan account via SMS, email, push notifications, or WhatsApp (where consented)
- Communicating policy changes, rate revisions, and important updates
- Sending promotional and marketing communications about Tata Capital products and offers (only with the customer's consent; the customer may opt out at any time)

### 3.5 Service Improvement & Analytics
- Analysing aggregated data to improve products, services, and customer experience
- Conducting customer satisfaction surveys
- Training machine learning models for credit assessment (using anonymised and aggregated data only)
- Internal reporting and business intelligence

Tata Capital processes personal data on the following legal bases:

| Basis | Description |
|-------|------------|
| **Consent** | The applicant provides explicit consent at the time of application. For digital applications, consent is obtained through a clickwrap agreement. For physical applications, a signed consent form is obtained. |
| **Contractual Necessity** | Processing is necessary for the performance of the loan agreement (e.g., disbursement, EMI collection, account servicing). |

## 5. Data Sharing & Disclosure

Tata Capital may share personal data with the following categories of recipients:

### 5.1 Credit Information Companies (CICs)
As mandated by RBI, borrower data (including loan details, repayment history, defaults, and closures) is reported to all four RBI-licensed CICs:
- TransUnion CIBIL Limited
- Experian Credit Information Company of India Pvt. Ltd.
- CRIF High Mark Credit Information Services Pvt. Ltd.
- Equifax Credit Information Services Pvt. Ltd.

This data forms part of the borrower's credit history and credit score.

### 5.2 Verification Agencies
- Employment verification agencies
- Address verification agencies (field investigation partners)
- Property valuation agencies and legal opinion providers
- Document authentication and fraud detection agencies

### 5.3 Service Providers & Partners
- Technology service providers (hosting, application management, data analytics)
- Payment processing partners (NPCI for NACH, banks for ECS)
- Insurance partners (for credit life insurance and property insurance, if opted by the borrower)
- Collection and recovery agencies (in case of default, engaged under strict RBI guidelines)
- Digital lending platform partners (where the application originates from a partner platform)

### 5.4 Group Companies
- Data may be shared within the Tata Group for cross-selling, analytical, and operational purposes, subject to applicable laws and the customer's consent/preferences. The customer may opt out of group-level marketing communications at any time.

### 5.5 Regulatory & Legal Authorities
- Reserve Bank of India (RBI)
- Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) — if applicable
- Income Tax Department, GST authorities
- Courts and tribunals
- Law enforcement agencies (Police, CBI, ED)
- Financial Intelligence Unit (FIU-IND) — under PMLA

### 5.6 Assignment / Securitisation
- In the event of loan assignment, securitisation, or portfolio sale, borrower data related to the assigned loan may be shared with the assignee, trustee, or special purpose vehicle, subject to regulatory requirements.

**Data will NOT be:**
- Sold to unrelated third parties for marketing purposes.
- Shared beyond what is permitted by law and regulatory guidelines.
- Used for any purpose not disclosed in this policy.

## 6. Data Security Measures

Tata Capital implements comprehensive security measures to protect personal data:

### 6.1 Technical Safeguards
- **Encryption**: Data is encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.2+) across all systems and communication channels.
- **Access Controls**: Role-based access control (RBAC) ensures that only authorised personnel can access personal data on a need-to-know basis.
- **Network Security**: Enterprise-grade firewalls, intrusion detection/prevention systems (IDS/IPS), and DDoS protection.
- **Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)**: Mandatory for all internal systems handling personal data.
- **Data Masking**: Sensitive data fields (Aadhaar, PAN, bank account numbers) are masked in application screens, reports, and logs.
- **Vulnerability Management**: Regular vulnerability assessments and penetration testing by independent security firms.

### 6.2 Organisational Safeguards
- **Data Protection Officer (DPO)**: Designated to oversee data protection compliance and respond to individual rights requests.
- **Employee Training**: Mandatory annual training on data privacy, information security, and phishing awareness for all employees and contractors.
- **Vendor Due Diligence**: All third-party service providers undergo security and privacy assessments before onboarding. Contractual obligations include data processing agreements with confidentiality, security, and breach notification clauses.
- **Incident Response**: Formal incident response plan in place for timely detection, containment, investigation, and notification of data breaches.
- **Audit**: Internal and external audits are conducted regularly. Findings are remediated within defined SLAs.

### 6.3 Physical Safeguards
- Data centres are ISO 27001 certified with biometric access, 24/7 surveillance, and environmental controls.
- Physical records are stored in secure, access-controlled facilities.
- Destruction of physical records is done through certified shredding services.

## 7. Data Retention Policy

| Data Category | Retention Period |
|--------------|-----------------|
| Active loan account data | Throughout the loan tenure + 8 years post closure |
| Rejected application data | 3 years from the date of rejection |
| Credit bureau data shared | Retained by CICs as per their policies and CICRA norms |
| KYC documents | As per RBI KYC Master Direction — minimum 5 years post account closure |
| Call recordings | 1–3 years from the date of the call |
| Digital analytics data (cookies, logs) | 1–2 years |
| Grievance records | 3 years from the date of resolution |

Data is securely destroyed at the end of the retention period using approved methods (cryptographic erasure for digital data, certified shredding for physical documents).

## 8. Individual Rights Under DPDPA 2023

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, individuals (Data Principals) have the following rights:

| Right | Description |
|-------|------------|
| **Right to Access** | Request a summary of personal data processed by Tata Capital, processing activities, and categories of third parties with whom data has been shared. |
| **Right to Correction & Erasure** | Request correction of inaccurate data or erasure of data that is no longer necessary for the purpose it was collected. Erasure is subject to regulatory retention obligations. |
| **Right to Grievance Redressal** | File a grievance with the Data Protection Officer if your rights are not adequately addressed. |
| **Right to Nominate** | Nominate another individual to exercise your rights in case of death or incapacity. |
| **Right to Withdraw Consent** | Withdraw consent for processing at any time. Note: withdrawal of consent for processing essential to loan servicing may result in inability to continue the loan facility. |

To exercise any of these rights, contact the Data Protection Officer using the details provided in Section 10.
